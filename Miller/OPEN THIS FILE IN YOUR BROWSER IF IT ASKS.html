The Millers

                  

State College Highschool

Both Granny and Papa attended State College High, but they were both missing when the time came for their JR. year photographs.

Interestingly enough, Papa was the more diplomatic once being involved in the Student government of the school, while Granny was involved in a little bit more of an intimidating genre of activities.

They both were involved in so many activities, and I was really wanting to find out who really was the most impressive of the two in highschool. It took a lot of researching to determine the winner but once i found the below information it really solidified that the most impressive of these two special people was determined on the basketball court.

Papa had been on the court for many years by the time they had reached State College High, but only one of them was an All-Star

Undergrad Penn State

While Papa attended Penn State for his undergraduate degree, Granny learned her curiosity in technology, accounting and typing(more on this shortly).

Graduate School Colorado School of Mines

After graduating, they made a big move out west and settled at the Colorado School of Mines where a Graduate Degree and PHD was earned.

Fun fact about the thesis titled Solvent Extraction of Metallic Anionic Complexes with a Quaternary Amine is that without the amazing skills Granny learned, Papa could potentially still be typing this thesis today....

Professor at University of Utah

Soon after graduation the family moved again to Salt Lake City to begin a job working at the University of Utah. After moving there Papa then collected the worlds largest collection of rocks and thought he struck gold but realized it was just Chalcopyrite.

However as one of his Grandchildren, visiting the largest rock collection in the world was always such an exciting experience that finding a diamond in a South Africa couldn't even measure up to.
